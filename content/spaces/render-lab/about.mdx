# Render Lab

Render Lab is a **shared space**, the first one on rendered_useful owned by a group instead of a single author. Two of us maintain it, and everything here is co-owned: the pages, the roadmap, the activity feed.

## Why a group space

Personal spaces answer "who are you?". A group space answers "what are we building together?". Features get prototyped here in the open instead of in private branches:

- Pages are MDX files in `content/spaces/render-lab/`, so either of us can PR a change
- The [Activity](/@render-lab/feed) page interleaves both members' posts automatically
- Each page carries its own byline (check the one at the top of this page and the [Roadmap](/@render-lab/roadmap))

## Members

- [Scott Peabody](/@scott-peabody): platform, design system, this feature
- [Ada Quill](/@ada-quill): performance, loaders, keeping Scott honest about bundle size

<Callout type="info" title="How it works">
  There's no auth and no backend. The group, its members, and this space are all declared in the same content registry as everything else. Resolution happens at `/@render-lab` by checking authors, then aliases, then groups. The build log is in [Groups in the @ Namespace](/articles/building-group-spaces).
</Callout>
