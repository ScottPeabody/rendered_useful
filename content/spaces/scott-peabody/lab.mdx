# Lab

This page overrides its space's theme (`cyberpunk`) and layout (`showcase`): proof that pages, not spaces, own their presentation. Navigate back to the [feed](/@scott-peabody) and the default theme snaps back.

## How a page becomes a page

    A["content/spaces/&lt;space&gt;/&lt;page&gt;.mdx"] --> B["import.meta.glob"]

## Components just work

Every component available to articles is available here, because space pages render through the same `MDXContentProvider`. For instance, an entire Rubik's cube:

## Why this matters

A space page isn't a template with slots. It's a document with the whole component library behind it. The difference between "profile customization" and "personal site".
